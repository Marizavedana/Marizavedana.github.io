About Me

I am a mosaic artist dedicated to creating vibrant, textured works that celebrate color, form, and artistic expression. With a passion for traditional techniques combined with contemporary design sensibilities, each piece tells a unique story.

My work draws inspiration from nature, cultural heritage, and the interplay of light and shadow. Whether creating intimate installations or large-scale commissions, I believe in the transformative power of mosaic art.

Based in Brazil, I continue to explore new possibilities in mosaic art while honoring the rich traditions of this ancient craft.
